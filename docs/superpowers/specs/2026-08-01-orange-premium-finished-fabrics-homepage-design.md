# O'range Premium Finished Fabrics Homepage Design

## Goal

Reposition the O'range Textile homepage from a double-knit-only entry page to a premium finished-fabric sourcing entry point that accurately covers finished knit and woven fabrics, custom development, and the wider greige-to-garment supply route.

## Approved Positioning

- Primary keyword: `finished fabric supplier`
- Homepage H1: `Finished Fabric Supplier for Premium Global Apparel Sourcing`
- Audience: global apparel brands, importers, buying teams, traders, and garment manufacturers seeking premium finished fabrics or specification-led development.
- Market signal: high-end, evidence-led fabric development and sourcing rather than a generic product catalogue.
- Category ownership: the homepage owns the broad finished-fabric query. Double knit remains a dedicated commercial category on `/finished-double-knit-fabrics`.

## Homepage Message

The hero must immediately communicate that O'range supplies finished knit and woven fabrics. It must not imply that double knit is the only product family.

Approved philosophy copy:

> We believe every successful collaboration begins with the belief that an idea is worth bringing to life.
>
> From an image, hand feel or garment brief, O'range Textile turns inspiration into premium finished fabric solutions through informed development, transparent communication and disciplined follow-through.

## Buyer Routes

The existing route chooser keeps its UI and becomes three distinct sourcing paths:

1. Finished knit fabrics: use the supplied A-J historical development archive and the existing 104-record knit catalogue as evidence of developed directions, without presenting every record as live stock.
2. Finished woven fabrics: introduce O'range's approved Jincang partner-supply scope using the supplied 26-product, five-chapter draft catalogue as category evidence. Do not publish missing specifications or concept imagery as product proof.
3. Custom fabric development: accept an image, reference sample, hand-feel target, garment brief, functional requirement, or early direction as the starting point for private development and quotation.

The second route can initially point to the existing custom inquiry route until a dedicated woven-fabric URL is approved and implemented. It must still be visibly distinct and descriptive on the homepage.

## Evidence Boundaries

- The A-J knit catalogue is a historical/developed-product archive, not a live-stock guarantee.
- The Jincang woven catalogue is approved for O'range public sales positioning, but remains a draft evidence source.
- Do not claim that concept images are product photography.
- Do not publish unconfirmed composition, width, GSM, treatment, MOQ, price, lead time, colour availability, certification, or inventory.
- Keep GRS language conditional on the applicable product and shipment documentation.
- Keep the verified `200+` circular knitting machine statement attributed to the parent-company record.

## SEO And Machine Readability

- Title starts with `Finished Fabric Supplier` and contains the brand once.
- Meta description naturally includes the complete primary keyword and covers knit, woven, development, sample review, and RFQ action.
- H1 contains the broad finished-fabric meaning and remains unique.
- Organization `knowsAbout`, website description, category ItemList name, entity facts, and homepage FAQ must reflect finished knit and woven fabrics.
- Existing dedicated knit and double-knit URLs remain unchanged to preserve their keyword ownership.

## UI Scope

Keep the existing homepage navigation, layout, hero component, colour system, cards, and interaction patterns. This round changes content hierarchy and buyer routing only. No redesign, new CMS, ecommerce flow, or route deletion is included.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The visible homepage does not use double knit as its H1 or sole summary subject.
- The exact approved philosophy appears as two visible sentences.
- Buyers can distinguish finished knit, finished woven, and custom development routes.
- The homepage SEO registry owns `finished fabric supplier` exactly once.
- `/finished-double-knit-fabrics` continues to own its specific double-knit commercial query.
- Structured data and machine-readable facts include knit and woven scope without unsupported claims.
- Existing tests, lint, typecheck, and production build pass.
